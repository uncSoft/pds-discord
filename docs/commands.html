Commands

Nine of them. Type / in any channel the bot can see and Discord lists them.

Asking

CommandWhat it does
/ask questionPuts your question to the library's assistant, which answers from the library's reviewed records and cites each one. Add private: true and only you see the answer.
Right-click a message → Apps → Ask the libraryTakes the question somebody already typed in chat and looks it up.
/forgetDrops the few turns kept in memory for your follow-up questions. Your next question starts fresh.

A follow-up in the same channel reads as a follow-up for ten minutes, so "and in women?" works after a question about men. Answers are shortened to fit Discord; a long one links to the rest.

Looking things up

CommandWhat it does
/search wordsFinds records by title, keyword, record ID or catalog number. Five at a time, each one a link.
/record idShows one record: what it is, who wrote it, where it appeared, and any warnings on it. Start typing and Discord suggests records.
/glossary termWhat a term means in the library, with a link to its page.
/newsThe most recent news items the library's curator has approved.

The rest

CommandWhat it does
/supportCrisis lines and the library's support page.
/aboutWhat the library is, what the bot will not do, and how to add it elsewhere.

What it will not answer

Questions about what to take, what dose, or whether something is safe for you are declined, in the assistant's own words. That is a decision made by the library's assistant, not something this bot could be talked out of. Nothing here is medical advice, and none of it replaces a clinician.
