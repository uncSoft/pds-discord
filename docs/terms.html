Not medical advice

This bot answers questions about published research on post-finasteride syndrome, post-SSRI sexual dysfunction and post-retinoid sexual dysfunction. It does not give medical advice, and it will not say what anyone should take, stop taking or try. It is not a clinician, a diagnosis or a treatment plan, and it is no substitute for care. Decisions about your health belong with you and the people treating you.

Answers are generated from the library's records by a language model. They can be wrong or incomplete. Every answer lists the records behind it: read them.

What the library is

A curated reading list chosen by patient community moderators, with summaries and context written for it. Records carry their own warnings where the source is retracted, unverifiable or not fully verified, and material from Dr Will Powers is attributed to him and marked as clinical theorizing rather than published evidence. The library is published under CC BY 4.0 and is citable by DOI; its terms are on its own site.

Using the bot

Add it to a server you are allowed to add bots to, and keep to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Do not use it to present its answers as medical advice to other people, to work around its refusal to give advice, or to flood it with automated questions. It limits how fast questions can be asked; a server that keeps hitting those limits may be blocked.
