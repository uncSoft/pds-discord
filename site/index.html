Research Library

Ask the post-drug syndromes research library from your own Discord server. Every answer cites the records it came from.

Add to Discord The library Commands

Answers with sources

/ask puts your question to the library's assistant. The reply lists the records it drew on, each one a link.

Research, not advice

Requests for dosing, diagnosis or what to take are declined. Dr Powers' views are attributed to him, and retracted or unverifiable sources are labeled.

The library itself

/search, /record and /glossary read the library directly, so you can look something up without asking a question.

Nothing stored

Your questions are not logged, here or by the assistant. A few recent turns stay in memory for follow-up questions, and /forget drops them.

Getting started

  1. Add the bot to your server. It asks for three permissions: see channels, send messages, embed links.
  2. Type /ask and your question. The answer appears in the channel, with its sources.
  3. Ask a follow-up in the same channel and it keeps the thread of the conversation for ten minutes.
  4. Right-click anyone's message and choose Apps, then Ask the library, to look up a question somebody asked in chat.

Server admins can limit the commands to particular channels in Server Settings, under Integrations. The bot never reads what anyone types: it only sees the questions people send it.

If you are struggling

These syndromes are hard to live with, and some of the people asking are in a bad way. Ask the bot anything about the research, but if you are in crisis please reach someone now: the library keeps the lines for several countries on its support page, and /support brings them up in Discord.
